## Pages 3–4
### The Big Announcement

Mrs. Harper stood beside a picture of a bright new fire station.

“Our town is building something special,” she said.

Leo nearly jumped from his seat.

“A fire station!”

Steph studied the picture.

“I like those big red doors.”

Maya noticed the drawings underneath it.

Ben raised his hand.

“Who plans a project that big?”

Vinny had another question.

“Do we get hard hats?”

Mrs. Harper smiled.

“You’re going to find out.”

---

## Pages 5–6
### Where Is the Fire Station?

The next morning, Mrs. Harper brought the Little Specs to the construction site.

They saw dirt.

They saw gravel.

They saw wooden stakes.

They saw orange fencing.

And Leo saw a giant excavator.

But there was one thing they did **not** see.

“A fire station,” Leo said. “Where is it?”

Steph held up the picture from class.

“This is definitely the right place.”

Ben studied the site.

“Maybe the building has already started,” he said.

“We just can’t see it yet.”

---

## Pages 7–8
### Meet the Project Team

A woman in a white hard hat walked toward them.

“I’m Ms. Rivera, the Project Manager,” she said. “My job is to help keep the whole project organized.”

Ben’s eyes went straight to the plans under her arm.

A man with gray hair and a radio stepped beside her.

“And I’m Mr. Jackson, the Superintendent. I help organize what happens out here every day.”

Leo stared at the excavator.

“So you know what all the machines are doing?”

Mr. Jackson grinned.

“I’d better.”

---

## Pages 9–10
### Safety Orientation

Before anyone went farther, Mr. Brooks gathered the group beside the visitor entrance.

“Every project starts with safety,” he said.

He showed them the rules.

Hard hats.

High-visibility vests.

Proper shoes.

Stay with the group.

Stay behind barricades.

Watch for moving equipment.

“Construction sites can be exciting,” Mr. Brooks said, “but we never let excitement make us forget safety.”

Leo was still watching the excavator.

He started forward before fastening his vest.

“Leo,” Vinny said.

Leo stopped.

Vinny pointed to the open vest.

“Oh.”

Leo fastened it.

Vinny nodded.

“Machines come after PPE.”

Mr. Brooks smiled.

“Good catch.”

---

## Pages 11–12
### The Fire Station Sticker

Ms. Rivera reached into a small box.

“There’s one more thing we give people when they join a project.”

She held up a small round sticker.

It showed the future fire station.

Across the bottom it read:

**FIRE STATION**

And beneath it was the Fire Station project emblem.

Leo’s eyes widened.

“For our hard hats?”

“For your hard hats,” Ms. Rivera said.

“This sticker represents the project you’re going to follow from the ground up.”

One by one, the kids placed their stickers wherever they wanted.

Ben carefully lined his up.

Maya checked hers twice before pressing it down.

Steph picked the spot that looked best.

Leo stuck his on quickly and grinned.

Vinny made sure his did not cover anything important.

Mr. Brooks nodded.

“Now remember where you put them. Project stickers stay with you.”

The kids looked at one another’s hard hats.

Their first project had officially begun.

## Pages 13–14
### A Building Made of Stakes

Nearby, a surveyor showed the kids small wooden stakes and bright marks painted on the ground.

“These tell us exactly where the building belongs,” she explained.

Maya looked surprised.

“A whole building from little sticks?”

“And very careful measurements,” said the surveyor.

She showed Maya how the distances on the site matched the numbers on the plan.

Maya checked one measurement.

Then another.

Her eyes narrowed.

She liked things to be exact.

---

## Pages 15–16
### Steph Sees It

Steph held the fire station drawing in front of her.

She looked at the empty dirt lot.

Then back at the drawing.

“There will be three big truck doors over there,” she said.

She pointed.

“And the entrance will be here.”

For a moment, Steph could almost see it.

Walls.

Windows.

Fire trucks.

Firefighters hurrying in and out.

Leo squinted.

“I still see dirt.”

Steph smiled.

“You have to imagine it first.”

---

## Pages 17–18
### Something Doesn’t Add Up

Maya looked at one of the layout points again.

Then she checked the plan.

Then the measurement.

Then the plan again.

“That’s strange.”

Ben walked over.

“What is?”

“This point should be farther away.”

Ben checked the drawing.

Steph looked toward the future fire-truck driveway.

“If the building moved that way,” she said, “wouldn’t the driveway get smaller?”

Maya nodded.

Ben looked toward Ms. Rivera.

“We should ask.”

---

## Pages 19–20
### Stop and Check

Ben approached Ms. Rivera.

“Maya found something we don’t understand.”

Ms. Rivera did not laugh.

She did not guess.

She said, “Show me.”

The surveyor checked the measurements.

Mr. Jackson inspected the stake.

Then he noticed tire tracks nearby.

“Aha.”

The stake had been bumped and was leaning away from its correct location.

The surveyor reset the point and checked again.

“Now it matches.”

Ms. Rivera smiled.

“Good question, team.”

---

## Pages 21–22
### One Little Stake

Dr. Patel joined them.

“What would happen if nobody checked it?” Maya asked.

Dr. Patel pointed toward the ground.

“If the foundation started in the wrong place, everything above it could be affected.”

“The walls?” asked Steph.

“Yes.”

“The driveway?” asked Ben.

“That too.”

Leo looked at the excavator.

“So even that giant machine could dig in the wrong place?”

Dr. Patel nodded.

Maya smiled.

“Measure twice. Build once.”

---

## Pages 23–24
### Digging Begins

Once the layout was checked, the excavator began to work.

Leo could hardly stand still.

The bucket dug into the soil.

Scoop.

Lift.

Swing.

Dump.

Mr. Jackson pointed toward the marks on the ground.

“The operator isn’t just digging anywhere. The crew follows the layout and the correct depth.”

Leo watched carefully.

“So the machine is huge…”

Mr. Jackson nodded.

“…but the measurements still have to be exact.”

Leo grinned.

“That makes it even cooler.”

---

## Pages 25–26
### What Goes Underground?

Soon, the kids saw pipes and conduits being placed below the future floor.

Leo tilted his head.

“Why put those down there now?”

Ms. Rivera pointed toward the area where concrete would eventually go.

“Because once we cover everything with concrete, getting underneath it becomes much harder.”

Ben looked at the schedule in her hands.

“So one job has to happen before another job can start.”

“Exactly,” she said.

Ben smiled.

Now **that** made sense.

---

## Pages 27–28
### Building the Foundation

Next came the reinforcing steel.

Maya studied the steel bars inside the forms.

Dr. Patel explained, “Concrete is very strong, and the steel helps it handle other forces too.”

Workers carefully checked the forms.

They checked the steel.

They checked the measurements.

Vinny noticed.

“They’re checking everything again?”

Mr. Brooks nodded.

“Once concrete covers it, we want to know the work underneath is right.”

Vinny approved.

“Checking twice sounds pretty safe to me.”

---

## Pages 29–30
### Concrete Day

Then came the day Leo had been waiting for.

A concrete truck rumbled onto the site.

“YES!” Leo whispered.

The Little Specs watched from behind the barricade, their Fire Station stickers now part of their hard hats.

Concrete filled the forms.

Workers guided it into place.

They smoothed it.

They checked it.

Slowly, the foundation began to appear.

Steph looked at her drawing.

Then at the concrete.

“I can see it now,” she said.

Ben smiled.

“It was there all along.”

“We just needed the plan.”

## Page 31
# Little Specs Construction Facts

### What is a foundation?
A foundation supports a building and helps transfer its weight safely into the ground.

### What does a surveyor do?
Surveyors use special tools and careful measurements to determine exactly where things should be built.

### What is rebar?
Rebar is reinforcing steel placed inside concrete to help make it stronger.

### Why do pipes sometimes go under the floor?
Some pipes and conduits must be installed before concrete covers the area.

### Why do construction workers put stickers on hard hats?
Project stickers can represent jobs, companies, training, milestones, or places a worker has been.

The Little Specs received their **Fire Station project sticker during safety orientation**, just like project stickers are often handed out at real jobsites.

### Vinny’s Safety Corner
Construction sites can be dangerous.

**Never enter an active construction site unless you are with an authorized adult and following the site’s safety rules.**
